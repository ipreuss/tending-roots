## About the Game

You are an Ordny, a traveling craftsperson who brings order to energy. After three years on the road you arrive in the Wurzeltal, a wide river valley where time is measured by what grows and people share what they have as gifts.

Every match **transforms energy**: Nourishing ripens berries, Flowing frees a blocked creek, Rooted strengthens young seedlings.

- **Story: "Arrival in the Wurzeltal".** You wake by the roadside and make your way to the village of Saatkern. Along the way you free a creek, help Thea with her flax, harvest carrots with Berin, spend an evening at Hagen's inn and bring in Marek's wheat. The game remembers how you answer.
- **Arcade: "Traumgarten".** Revisit story fields as dream memories: new seeds every morning and a best score to beat. Each field opens up once you've experienced it in the story.
- **Five energy states:** Resting, Nourishing, Flowing, Rooted and Changing, each with its own effect on the world.
- **Every outcome carries the story forward.** Each scene has a goal, and whatever you harvest becomes part of your journey.
- **Your own pace.** Turn-based play: every move waits until you're ready.
- **Colored-pencil art style** and a layered soundtrack that follows the day.
- **Play your way:** touch, mouse, keyboard or gamepad, in English or German, with adjustable text size, a dyslexia-friendly font, reduced motion and more.
